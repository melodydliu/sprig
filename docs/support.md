# Sprigbook — Support

Sprigbook is a personal foraging journal for iOS: photograph a plant the moment
you spot it, note what it is and how much there is, and find it again later by
list or map.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
Yes — an email and password. Your journal is stored on your device and works
fully offline; the account is what lets you back it up and restore it on a new
phone.

**Where are my photos and notes stored?**
On your device first. When you're signed in they're also copied to a private
cloud backup that only your account can read. Nothing is sold or shared. See the
[Privacy Policy](./privacy).

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete a single find from inside the app. To remove everything — finds, photos,
notes, profile, from both the device and the cloud — use
**Settings → Delete account**.

**I didn't get the confirmation or password-reset email.**
Check spam first. If it still hasn't arrived after a few minutes, email the
address below and I'll sort it out.
